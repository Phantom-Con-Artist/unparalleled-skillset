# An authored product experience

Premium is a consequence of decisions that belong together: clear intent, distinctive forms, exact relationships, convincing assets, responsive behavior, and care in the places people actually touch. It is not a color scheme, a pricing claim, an adjective to repeat in copy, or a requirement to make everything quiet. A vivid, playful, or technically dense product can feel exceptionally crafted.

This foundation synthesizes methods from Impeccable, Awwwards, and Taste without importing their presets. Read [source-methods.md](source-methods.md) for the selection and boundaries. Read [reference-intentions.md](reference-intentions.md) when a brief seeks an expressive, flagship experience. Every installed skill includes these references; no other skill or proprietary tool is required.

## Determine the purpose of this surface

| Purpose | Successful experience | Where ambition belongs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choose | A visitor understands the offer, trusts its evidence, and takes a relevant next step. | Product demonstration, compelling composition, useful comparison, and a direct action. |
| Explore | A visitor discovers a work, world, object, or story. | An authored environment, meaningful agency, spatial progression, and a clear way through and out. |
| Work | A person completes a real task repeatedly with confidence. | Information hierarchy, precise tools, trustworthy feedback, consistent states, and useful shortcuts. |
| Read | A person understands or finds information. | Reading rhythm, structure, diagrams, search, and wayfinding. |

Choose per surface, not per company. A software landing page and its working dashboard have different purposes. Separate purpose from change scope: a local refinement inherits the established world; a requested overhaul replaces its visual language while preserving facts, useful capabilities, and explicit commitments. Missing design documentation is not evidence that no identity exists.

## Establish a direction contract

For a new surface or overhaul, write the contract in the prospective direction or surface brief before substantial implementation. For a local refinement, inherit the contract and keep only a short note of the changed intent and checks in the existing record; do not initiate a new direction exercise. Keep it concise enough to use while building. Do not create duplicate documents when the project already has a home for these decisions.

| Decision | What must be concrete |
| --- | --- |
| Product truth | Who is here, what they need, what is actually offered, the main action, and the proof available. |
| Particular inventory | Specific objects, processes, constraints, language, places, outputs, or data relationships from this project. Seek 8–12 when evidence supports them; never invent details to meet a count. |
| Governing idea | Explain how one particular product truth changes the composition or interaction. An adjective, a genre name, and a moodboard are insufficient. |
| Composition | What owns the first frame, what anchors orientation, how scale and density change, and how later sections or panes relate. |
| Visual grammar | Distinctive type behavior, palette evidence and proportions, geometry, marks, material cues, image crops, and where precision or irregularity belongs. |
| Signature behavior | A working interaction that reveals, compares, transforms, or helps someone do something specific to the product, including its touch, keyboard, and static equivalent. |
| Asset route | Which assets exist, which will be authored, their rights and credibility, and how the concept survives a missing or delayed asset. |
| Journey and continuity | Arrival, orientation, exploration or work, proof or feedback, action, exit, and return. Identify the same visual/behavioral rule in more than the first screen. |
| Implementation boundary | Actual stack, device/input constraints, dependency choices, scene budgets, and acceptance evidence. |

## Originality needs substance

Run the competitor-swap test: if the closest competitor could use the same concept by changing a name, a color, and an image, make the idea more specific. Shared operational conventions are not a failure: a table, filter, or familiar editing pattern can be exactly right. The test asks whether the product-specific hierarchy and behavior have been considered, not whether every control is unprecedented. With sparse evidence, name a provisional idea and strengthen it from actual content rather than demanding a fabricated exclusive story. Do not counterfeit specificity with invented addresses, founder memories, metrics, or technical details. When a generic brief offers little evidence, identify assumptions in the artifact and choose an honest product mechanism rather than a fictional company history.

Removing cards, borders, labels, or gradients is not enough. Replace the template with a compelling focal element, full useful content, a real demonstration, or a legible operational relationship. Empty space must clarify attention or action. A stripped, tiny-content page is not premium merely because it avoids obvious tells.

## Craft operates across scales

At the largest scale, commit to an original composition and a clear attention path. At the middle scale, connect sections, panes, scenes, and objects through a consistent grammar. At the smallest scale, resolve glyph fit, baseline alignment, line length, image crops, control shape, hit area, selection, focus, pending state, and error recovery. The best detail cannot rescue an unchosen composition; a bold composition does not excuse defective controls.

Treat type as both voice and reading infrastructure. Establish the actual family, available assets, license, scripts, numeral forms, fallback metrics, and optical sizes. Use calculated fluid scales and font-specific spacing. A stock fashion font is not bespoke by association. Check long content and small labels as seriously as the headline.

Use material behavior selectively and consistently. A real object can reflect, scatter, occlude, bend, or show wear. Interface chrome should not pretend to be that material everywhere. Choose a lighting rule and depth order when the scene needs them. Grain, bloom, chromatic aberration, blur, noise, and refraction must each earn their computational and reading cost.

## Continuity and the human hand

The same product must be recognizable after the hero, inside the main task, on a phone, in a failure state, and on return. Do not spend all invention on an opening and finish with stock features, generic pricing, or unstyled forms. Custom marks, shared silhouettes, carried objects, type rhythm, and interaction logic can establish continuity without repeating one motif everywhere.

Discreet imperfection is authored character: an asymmetrical silhouette, slightly varied cadence, or an annotation that follows a real action. Apply it with intent, not random jitter or a distressed finish. Keep reading order, measurements, control alignment, facts, and accessibility reliable.

## Build, look, correct, finish

Prototype the hardest signature relationship early enough to change the plan. Build a complete path through the real content and primary action before cosmetic decoration. Inspect the rendered first frame, full experience, narrow phone, intermediate width, short laptop viewport, and motion-off state together. Review the same path with keyboard and touch. Batch the important corrections into a focused craft pass.

Correct the source of a flaw: a bad token at the token, inconsistent component behavior at the shared component, a weak hierarchy in the composition. Do not add local overrides until the screenshot happens to look right. Reinspect changed scenarios. Finish when the direction is evident, the main action works, evidence passes, and remaining differences are subjective preferences. No endless perfection loop and no invented claim of surpassing every reference.
