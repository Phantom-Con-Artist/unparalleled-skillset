# Methods blended, presets rejected

These notes were synthesized from the installed Impeccable 4.7.0, Awwwards, and Taste v2 skills reviewed on 25 October 2026. They describe adopted methods in original language. They are not copied manuals, dependencies, endorsements, or promises that combining skill names guarantees a particular quality level. Their full catalogs and scripts are not redistributed.

| Source | Method retained | Deliberate departure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impeccable | Distinguish a working tool from a persuasive page or exploratory experience; preserve product truth; handle full states; review the rendered result; fix a problem at its system level. | No fixed-count aesthetic tournament; new directions use a concrete specimen review with explicit user green light unless waived. No random catalog assignment, blanket illustration ban, or blanket ban on forms such as serif type or borders. |
| Awwwards | Establish the concept before technology; make typography, rhythm, interaction, and performance one creative decision; give mobile an intentional composition. | No aesthetic genre catalog, compulsory smooth-scroll library, custom cursor, theatrical preloader, fixed hero formula, or official-award claim. |
| Taste | Inventory the particular subject; connect claims to evidence; test the concept against a competitor; make the central live interaction useful; carry identity across the whole page or task; look at screenshots and real content density. | No preset dealing, default Inter/Geist stack, fixed image quota, compulsory imagery percentage, data-color quota, hard two-line heading limit, or statistical median promoted into universal law. |

When an adopted method conflicts with the user's actual brief, verified identity, or access requirements, the brief and requirements win. Preserve Unparalleled's bans on emoji decoration, visible bullet-point inventories, stock palettes, genre recipes, generic sans identities, buzzword filler, and em-dash spam. Named reference sites are evidence of ambition and authored intent, never component libraries to transplant.

## Integration without runtime coupling

An agent can execute either Unparalleled skill without Impeccable, Awwwards, Taste, a specific browser package, or a particular image service installed. Use whatever equivalent tools are actually available. A tool's absence changes the implementation route or the evidence reported, not the truthfulness of the claims.

External research can inform a decision, but its categories and measurements are not automatically requirements. For example, an admired site may use canvas-only text, an entrance gate, a copied brand typeface, or a long pinned journey. Keep the design intent while providing semantic access, direct navigation, readable content, and measured performance in the new work.
