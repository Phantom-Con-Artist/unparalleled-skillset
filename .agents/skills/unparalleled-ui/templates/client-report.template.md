# Message to the user

Write this after the contract record, in the user's words. The record's ids, state names and gate verdicts stay in the record. Translate each into the thing it stands for: not "F3" but "the brief says commissions take 12 to 16 weeks"; not "execute gate failed" but "I can't start building yet because ...". Keep every list numbered and complete. Replace each line below with what you actually found; delete a section only when it truly has nothing to say.

## What I understood you asked for

Their request in their own words, then what they explicitly chose (stack, brand, bans, dimension, review waiver) and what each choice does and does not cover.

## What I found

What the repository and notes actually show, with the file for each fact. Then what is missing or unopened, named plainly (for example "the photographs are listed but not in the folder").

## What I propose

The governing idea in two sentences. Why a competitor's name dropped in would break it. Each vague word they used (premium, clean, cool, synced) as a decision they can check. Mark anything that rests on a guess as a guess.

## What I need from you

Only the questions that change the work, each with what I will do meanwhile if there is no answer.

## What happens next

1. Each step in order, with how you and I will tell it worked.
2. What must be approved or verified before building starts, and by whom.

## Where things stand

Ready to build, or not, and the one reason in plain words. List anything not yet checked and why.
