# Product surface brief

## Truth and scope

Describe the product, user situation, screen purpose, primary task, data sources, error consequences, and authorized change scope. Separate established facts, brand commitments, and assumptions.

## Stack and production pipeline

Record existing constraints, the recommended framework/runtime, product-specific rationale, one credible alternative/tradeoff, and any user-selected technology. Declare 2D/3D/hybrid and interactive/scroll/audio/rendered motion. Name each library's responsibility, client/server boundary, target budgets and fallback. Use the framework and pipeline references in this skill; retain the existing stack for local refinements.

For 3D: record open-model queries/candidates, inspected reference views, sources/licenses, reuse/adapt/scratch decision, verified Blender MCP readiness or pending setup, and any explicit alternative authorized by the user. Record generated cinematic studies and their role separately from factual geometry. For motion graphics: record storyboard, kinetic-text reading windows, output fps/dimensions/duration, audio source/rights, editable cue map, trim/offset, clock authority and sync verification. Include only applicable fields; append actual evidence after the checks occur.

## Governing idea

Explain which particular product relationship becomes the information hierarchy and signature working interaction. Record why a competitor-name swap fails. Describe alternatives only when the structure is open; preserve the established world for a narrow refinement.

## Direction contract

Describe first-frame composition, main work surface, density, distinctive readable type, palette evidence and roles, bespoke marks, component geometry, and asset route. Explain how these remain recognizable in the phone layout and important states.

## Motion and spatial work

Describe the relationship clarified by any movement or 2D/3D inspection, explicit controls, interruption rules, performance plan, reduced-motion behavior, and honest renderer-failure limits. Do not invent a scene where the task needs a conventional form.

## Acceptance evidence

Record the end-to-end task, actual contrast pairs, content/data extremes, responsive and short-height captures, input checks, runtime/asset failures, and relevant product tests. Include review findings and completion limits after performing the work.

## Specimen review and approved baseline

Record the recommended design choices, optional alternatives, one representative task specimen URL/file and current revision. Keep a concise feedback/change/evidence ledger with draft/awaiting review/changes requested/approved state. Record explicit user green light and scope, or their explicit review waiver, before full new-surface development. Local fixes inherit the existing direction. Compare final work with this baseline.
