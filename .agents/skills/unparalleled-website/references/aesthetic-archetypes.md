# Aesthetic Archetypes for Websites

Select one of these five proven aesthetic directions to guide typography, palette, texture, and layout.

---

## 1. Linear / Vercel Dark Tech
- **Ideal For**: Developer tools, AI platforms, cloud infrastructure, hardware engineering.
- **Palette**: Obsidian canvas (`#09090b`), translucent hairline borders (`border-white/10`), crisp white typography, neon electric punch (Emerald, Cyan, Indigo).
- **Typography**: Geist Sans + Geist Mono.
- **Hero**: Asymmetrical split or centered terminal preview with radial ambient lighting.

## 2. Editorial Luxe / Artisan Craft
- **Ideal For**: Luxury goods, architecture, premium consultancies, high-end publications.
- **Palette**: Warm alabaster (`#faf7f2`), espresso charcoal (`#1a1412`), muted terracotta or olive bronze.
- **Typography**: Newsreader or Instrument Serif paired with Satoshi or Inter.
- **Hero**: Expansive full-bleed photography, elegant serif headlines, generous margin breathing space.

## 3. Swiss / International Typographic
- **Ideal For**: Design studios, cultural institutions, architecture, research labs.
- **Palette**: Stark architectural grays or crisp off-white (`#f2f2f0`), International Klein Blue (`#002fa7`) or Swiss Red (`#ff2b00`).
- **Typography**: Heavy grotesque (Neue Haas Grotesk, PP Neue Montreal) set monumentally large with tight letterspacing (`-0.035em`).
- **Hero**: High-contrast oversized typographic grid, zero drop shadows, crisp line dividers.

## 4. Neo-Brutalism / High-Contrast Tactile
- **Ideal For**: Creative agencies, indie developers, events, consumer apps, newsletters.
- **Palette**: Saturated pastels or pure white canvas with solid 2–3px black borders and hard offset drop shadows (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #000`).
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk, Syne, Archivo Black with JetBrains Mono.
- **Hero**: Floating sticker badges, bold marquee tickers, chunky tactile buttons.

## 5. Warm Organic / Humanist Modern
- **Ideal For**: Wellness, climate tech, sustainability, community platforms, artisan food.
- **Palette**: Clay, sand, sage, or oatmeal (`#f5f2eb`, `#e9ece5`) with forest moss and terracotta accents.
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit, DM Sans.
- **Hero**: Soft rounded cards (`20px`), gentle diffused shadows, calm natural photography.
