# Poterie Céleste specimen site

Scope: a multi-page React app in `site/apps/poterie-celeste` (home, collections, collection, piece, commissions, atelier, journal, journal entry, visit, not found), built into `site/public/poterie-celeste/` for Cloudflare Pages. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: collectors and interior designers deciding whether to acquire a listed piece or commission one. Action: an enquiry, appointment or commission brief composed as an email to the specimen address `atelier@poterieceleste.com`. Constraints: no prices, addresses, press, or testimonials; the footer states the atelier, its people, words and photography are fictional.

## Direction contract

The user pinned this direction on 2026-10-26 and it replaces the earlier kiln-firing assignment.

THESIS: One real vessel, modelled in Blender, stands centre stage in a spotlit plaster studio and breaks apart as the visitor scrolls: whole, shattering, floating, mending. It refuses the category default of a product grid under a hero photograph; the object itself is the opening.

OWN-WORLD: A warm stone studio: cracked putty wall (Poly Haven `concrete_wall_003`, high-passed and toned), a worn stucco plinth one step darker than the pot, a matte speckled stoneware pot as the brightest thing in frame, one warm key spotlight and a soft wall pool. Inner pages sit on putty `#efebe5` with umber ink `#2b1f19` from the wordmark and iron `#7a5a42` from the clay speckle; each collection page takes its own glaze field. Type is Brygada 1918 for the voice and Familjen Grotesk for labels. Controls are square with a hairline rim set inside, like a gold lip.

STORY: The visitor meets the pot whole, watches it break and hang in the light while short chapters explain why each piece is made once, sees it mend, walks the collections and heritage glazes, designs a commission in 3D, and at the bottom meets the same studio turned upside down like a reflection, breaking again before the owner's portrait and quote close the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed live WebGL studio, pot and plinth centred. Left: "Vessels made once." in light Brygada over a dark scrim, two sentences, "Walk the collections" and "Commission a piece". Right: a museum label for the Céleste Vessel. Bottom centre: chapter rail and "Scroll, and it breaks". A matching poster still covers loading and no-WebGL.
