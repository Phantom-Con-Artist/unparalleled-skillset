# Claude, in one line: a prospective design

## Product truth and scope
Subject: Claude, the AI model made by Anthropic, selling itself. Audience: someone deciding whether to hand real work to an assistant. Primary action: open claude.ai (link only; this page has no account, pricing or sign-up of its own). Only verifiable facts appear: Anthropic makes Claude; it works in chat, in Claude Code (terminal and editor) and through an API; it reads text, images and PDFs; it writes and runs code; it can be wrong and does not carry memory across conversations unless a memory feature is on. No metrics, customers, testimonials or prices. The four "handoffs" are scripted illustrations and are labeled so. The page is an independent demo, not an official Anthropic site.

## Governing idea
Work usually reaches me as a tangle. The whole page is one coral line. It starts as a scribble in the first frame and straightens as you scroll, and the same line becomes the spine that threads every later section. Competitor-swap test: another assistant could not reuse this, because the mechanism shown (messy input in, visible working, made thing out) is drawn as one continuous strand and the copy speaks in the first person about my actual limits.

## Vocabulary
tangle, strand, knot, loop, slack, tension, pull, thread, handoff, trace, draft, hold, snag, frayed end, splice, taut, unpick, stitch, spine.

## First frame
Left-low: the headline "Hand me the tangle." in a warm, slightly irregular display serif at clamp(3.5rem, 11vw, 10rem). A coral scribble occupies the upper right and bleeds off the frame. One plain sentence says what I am. Two actions: try a handoff (scrolls), open Claude (external).

## Visual grammar
Type: Young Serif (display, quirk in the counters echoes the hand-drawn line) with Literata (reading text, traces). Georgia fallback. Color from the subject: cool graph-paper blue-grey (oklch 0.955 0.012 235), blue-black ink (0.2 0.03 255), Claude coral for the line (0.66 0.17 40) and a deeper coral for text roles (0.5 0.16 38). One night section for the limits. Geometry: a single stroke weight, round caps, one slightly rotated paper scrap for messy input, straight edges for finished output. Marks: four SVG glyphs drawn as the same line doing something (threading, forking, looping, prompting).

## Signature behavior
1. Hero strand: scroll progress interpolates ~16 control points from scribble to a taut line (left end relaxes first). Reduced motion shows the settled middle state.
2. Handoff: choose a tangle (bug, email, contract, blank page), press Hand it over, watch the working trace appear line by line, then the made result. Tabs are keyboard operable; reduced motion prints everything at once.

## Journey
Arrival (tangle) > Handoff demo (proof) > How I work (method on the spine) > Where to find me (surfaces, model names) > Where I fall short (night section, honest) > Bring the tangle (action) > disclosure footer.

## Assets
None external. All art is authored inline SVG and CSS. Fonts from Google Fonts with local serif fallbacks; page remains complete without them.

## Alternatives considered (not built as live specimens)
Five hypotheses were weighed on paper: (1) the strand (chosen), (2) a conversation transcript that rewrites itself, (3) a workbench of tools laid out as a plan drawing, (4) a margin-notes review of a document, (5) a terminal-first scroll. The strand won on clarity of product, distinct silhouette and feasibility without WebGL. A live five-specimen page was not produced in this run; this is a stated gap.

## Acceptance evidence
First frame, mid-scroll, demo after run, narrow phone, short laptop, reduced-motion render, keyboard path through tabs and button, contrast checks on text roles.
