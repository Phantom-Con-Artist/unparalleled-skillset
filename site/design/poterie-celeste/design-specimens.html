A house of rooms, one glaze to each

The page is a line of salons seen through their doorways. Each room holds one collection in the light of its photograph. Visitors walk the enfilade sideways or step through with the door controls.

Fit 81 of 100.Product truth 25: the photographs are rooms, so the structure is honest. Audience 23: designers think in rooms. Originality 15: a gallery of salons sits close to the category's own luxury default. Feasibility 18: plain scroll-snap with buttons.

Colour arrives in the fire

Glaze goes onto the pot as a dull powder. The cobalt, the gold, and the cranes only appear once the kiln reaches heat. The page lets every piece arrive that way, and lets a commission be fired before it is sent.

Recommended. Fit 86 of 100.Product truth 27: firing is how every one of these glazes exists. Audience 19: collectors know the result better than the kiln, so the heat stays a framing device rather than studio lore. Originality 23: the colour fields, cooling, and firing control belong to these glazes alone. Feasibility 17: filters and blend layers, one-shot.
