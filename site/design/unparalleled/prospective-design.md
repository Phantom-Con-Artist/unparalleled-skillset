# Unparalleled website: prospective direction

## Product truth, purpose, and scope

Unparalleled is two agent skills (`unparalleled-website`, `unparalleled-ui`) and a dependency-free installer for twelve AI coding agents. Surface purpose: Choose. A developer or designer should understand what changes in their agent's output, see proof, and copy `npx github:Phantom-Con-Artist/unparalleled-skillset install all`. Scope: the root page of the Pages site plus the demo links. Facts are in `site/apps/unparalleled/PRODUCT.md`.

## Particular inventory and research

The capture script's twelve measurable flags (pill shapes, similar framed cards, kanban columns, default typefaces, tiny tracked uppercase labels, ornamental status dots, stock SaaS hues, dark slate plus neon, emoji, em-dash density, horizontal overflow, runtime errors); the five-width screenshot loop (desktop 1440, laptop 1366x768, tablet 834, phone 390, narrow 320) plus reduced motion; the direction contract; five hypotheses with fit scores out of 100 (truth 30, audience 25, originality 25, feasibility 20); the twelve agents and their native skill folders; the installer's flags (`--global`, `--agent`, `--dry-run`, `--all-agents`, `--no-config`); `scaffold ui|website`; the three demos.

Research limit: no competitor browsing in this run. Category knowledge (agent-skill and design-tool landing pages favour dark terminal heroes and feature-card rows) is general.

## Governing idea

The site is a toile. A generic generated page is pinned up as unbleached muslin; the capture loop marks each tell on it in chalk; the visitor applies the corrections one by one and the page is re-cut. Then three real sites, cut in cloth, prove it. A competitor could not swap names in: the marks are this product's own flag list, the fitting is its own screenshot loop, and the finished garments are sites built with it.

## Direction contract

See `site/apps/unparalleled/.impeccable/surfaces/site-apps-unparalleled-src-app-jsx.md` for THESIS, OWN-WORLD, STORY and FIRST VIEWPORT.

Palette evidence: kraft pattern paper (ground), printer's black (text), tailor's chalk blue (corrections), red tacking thread (the active mark), tape-measure yellow (the command), unbleached muslin (the generic page under fitting). Proportions: kraft owns most of the page, muslin owns the fitting, blue and red stay in marks, yellow appears only on the command.

Type: Archivo (OFL, variable weight and width) condensed and heavy for headings and the wordmark, normal width for reading; Martian Mono (OFL) only for commands and paths. Marks: notches, grainline arrows, dotted cutting lines and tailor's crosses drawn as SVG at fixed stroke weights. No handwriting faces: the brief asks for craft, not hand-drawn material.

## Assets, voice, and signature prototype

Assets: screenshots of the three demos captured from the real builds; everything else code-native. Voice: plain and specific, the way a maintainer explains their tool.

Signature: the fitting. A generic Poterie Céleste landing page (hero kit, indigo pill buttons, eyebrow label, three cards, Inter, a status dot) is rendered as muslin. Six chalk corrections, each named after a real capture flag, are applied by scrolling or by pressing "Fit next". Each correction changes the page live. Keyboard: buttons; static: before and after shown side by side with the six notes.
